      The Desk’s View

      Ship Small, Correct Loudly

      This newspaper was founded on a modest proposition: that a person who builds things should write down what they learned while it is still slightly embarrassing. Most of what appears in these pages concerns small tools made for one user. That is not a limitation. Small tools are where the craft is easiest to see.

      We make two promises in return for your attention. We will say plainly what worked and what did not. And when we get something wrong, we will correct it in the same type, on the same page, without adjectives. The corrections box is not a formality. It is the most honest column we print.

      Readers will notice that the paper’s opinions are, for now, all held by the same person. The editorial board has discussed this at length and found itself in complete agreement.

      Unsigned editorials represent the view of the desk.

      §3

      Texture

      Rest your cursor anywhere on the page for a moment and the ink will begin to smudge.

      Newsprint

      Fractal noise at two scales over a long-fibre turbulence. Stock #F3EEE2, never pure white.

      Halftone Screen

      Dot area follows darkness. Key plate at 45°, spot plate at 15°, misregistered by a hair.

      Stipple Hedcut

      Portraits are drawn in seeded random dots, dense in shadow and sparse in light, in the manner of the old dot-and-line engravers.

      Ink
      Ink Bleed

      A displacement filter roughens the nameplate and banner headlines the way wet ink spreads into cheap paper.

      REGISTER
      Misregistration

      Cyan and red plates sit a fraction off the key. Hover any headline and the press drifts further.

      Fold
      The Fold

      A crease of shadow and highlight. On the front page, the top half tips away as you read below it.
