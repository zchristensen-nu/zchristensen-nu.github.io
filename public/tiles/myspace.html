Z.'s Latest Blog Entries [Subscribe to this Blog]

  • Building a comment overlay for prototypes (Sep 2026)

    I kept screenshotting my own localhost and pasting it into chat with arrows drawn on top. So I built a proxy that sits in front of any dev server, injects a tiny overlay, and lets me drop a pin right on the thing that's wrong. Each pin saves the URL, a screenshot, and what I typed. Then I just say “address my feedback.”
    Currently mood: accomplished

  • What I learned shipping a daily news digest agent (Aug 2026)

    Lesson one: the agent is the easy part. The hard part is deciding what NOT to include, and writing that down so clearly that a machine can follow it at 6am. It runs before I'm awake now, and I've stopped checking whether it did. That's the whole review.
    Currently mood: well-rested

  • Notes on designing for motion (Jun 2026)

    Motion is a sentence, not a word. Things that move should tell you where they came from and where they went. And always ship a reduced-motion path, even for the sparkles. Especially for the sparkles.
